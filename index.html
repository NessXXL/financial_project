How it works

Two engines. One honest signal.

No black box. Every number in the output can be traced back to a real headline or a real line in the financial statements.

📰

1 · Read the news

Live headlines are pulled for the ticker. Our custom BiLSTM + CNN + Attention model — trained from scratch on 33,000 labeled financial sentences — classifies each one as positive, neutral or negative.

score = mean(P(pos) − P(neg)) → 0–100
📋

2 · Read the books

All 35 ratios — liquidity, profitability, leverage, efficiency, valuation, cash flow — are computed from real statements and graded against published benchmarks: green, watch or warning.

ratio_score = (2·green + 1·watch) / max → 0–100
🎯

3 · Combine — honestly

Fundamentals dominate at 65%, news adds real-time context at 35%. No generated text, no bias boosters, no thumb on the scale. ≥70 BUY · ≥45 HOLD · below SELL.

0.35 × sentiment + 0.65 × ratios

Methodology

Why 35 / 65?

We stress-tested every weight split on real tickers — strong (META), expensive (NVDA), broken (FUBO). Only one split stayed honest in all three cases.

100 / 0
Pure news. Ignores financials completely — a near-bankrupt company with nice headlines gets a BUY. Dangerous.
50 / 50
Neutral headlines pull fundamentally broken companies (like FUBO, 15 red ratios) up into HOLD territory.
35 / 65 ✓
Balanced. Fundamentals dominate, news adds context. FUBO stays SELL, META stays BUY, NVDA gets an honest HOLD.
30 / 70
Nearly identical to 35/65. Acceptable for a more conservative profile.
0 / 100
Pure fundamentals. Blind to breaking news and momentum — misses what the market is saying right now.
